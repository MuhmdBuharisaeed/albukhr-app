1. Purpose and service scope

ALBUKHR may assist applicants with preparing, designing, developing or launching a Pi Network decentralised application (dApp), depending on the selected service and the scope set out in an accepted quote.

  • Pi Studio Assistance: guidance for preparing and launching a dApp through Pi Studio.
  • Developer-built dApp: a request for custom development by ALBUKHR developers or contractors.
  • App for an ALBUKHR Project: available only for eligible Internal or External projects after the service verifies the applicant’s ownership and project eligibility.

Core projects are managed by the ALBUKHR Team and are not selectable through the applicant-owned project pathway. Manager access is not implied unless explicitly enabled and verified.

2. Request submission and review

Applicants must provide accurate information, including the app or project name, requirements, selected service route and the required policy consent.

  • ALBUKHR verifies the applicant’s Pi identity on the server side.
  • Project ownership and network scope must be validated by the service. A claim sent by the browser is not proof of identity or ownership.
  • Administrators may request more information, decline a request or determine that the requested scope is not feasible.
  • Submitting a request does not mean it is approved, funded, queued or ready for work.

3. Quote-first workflow

The intended workflow is: submit a request, administrative review, quote issuance, then the applicant’s decision on the specific quote version. The trusted service must confirm each applicable status.

  1. Submit: the applicant submits a request and accepts the identified policy version.
  2. Review: ALBUKHR assesses requirements, feasibility and any linked project eligibility.
  3. Quote: if appropriate, ALBUKHR issues a quote stating scope, deliverables, price, currency or asset, validity and applicable terms.
  4. Decision: the applicant may accept or decline that exact quote version. Acceptance is not payment confirmation.

A change to scope or price requires a revised quote/version; an issued quote must not be silently altered. Work may begin only after all required service, payment and operational approvals are confirmed under the final published workflow.

4. Fees and payment status

This policy does not set a fixed price or enable a payment method. The applicable quote and separately approved terms must state any fee, currency or asset, payment method, validity period and payment conditions.

  • Submitting a request or accepting a quote must not be represented as a completed payment.
  • Payment status is separate from the request or work status.
  • Pi payments, Pi U2A and bank transfer are not enabled by this policy. A channel may be used only after ALBUKHR separately approves, tests and explicitly activates it.
  • Do not send funds to a personal wallet or bank account based only on a chat message, screenshot or unverified instruction.

5. Cancellation, refunds and timelines

Detailed cancellation rules, refund eligibility and timing, revision limits, delivery acceptance, turnaround commitments and dispute procedures are not finalized by this policy. They must be stated in separately approved terms or the applicable quote before the applicant commits.

Applicants must not assume that a payment is refundable or non-refundable unless the final applicable terms expressly say so. ALBUKHR must not promise an unauthorized refund schedule or delivery date.

6. Ownership and responsibilities

Applicants remain responsible for the accuracy and legality of the content, branding, data, permissions and third-party materials they provide. Providing assistance does not automatically make ALBUKHR the owner of an applicant’s dApp.

Ownership and licensing of source code, intellectual property, credentials, hosting, maintenance, handover and third-party materials must be expressly agreed in the final written scope or terms. This policy alone does not transfer intellectual property or grant an unstated licence.

7. Pi Network and third-party decisions

ALBUKHR may provide assistance within the agreed scope, but cannot guarantee acceptance, listing, visibility, approval or continued availability by Pi Network or any third party.

  • Pi Network Core Team approval or listing is not guaranteed.
  • User adoption, revenue, investment returns and Pi earnings are not guaranteed.
  • Applicants must comply with applicable Pi Network rules, security requirements and relevant laws.

8. Security, identity and records

Requests should be associated with the server-verified Pi identity and the network configured by the service. Applicants must not attempt to access another person’s requests, quotes or private service information.

ALBUKHR may retain request, quote, consent and administrative audit records for service delivery, security, dispute review and compliance. Never include passwords, private keys, wallet recovery phrases, Pi access tokens or other authentication secrets in request descriptions or messages.

9. Misuse and refusal

ALBUKHR may pause, decline or close a request if information is misleading, the request is unlawful or unsafe, the applicant cannot establish the required authority, the scope is technically infeasible, or processing would conflict with ALBUKHR policy or applicable third-party rules. Where appropriate, a reason may be provided through the service workflow.

10. Policy acceptance, changes and status

When an applicant accepts this policy and submits a request, the consent record should identify the policy version accepted. Consent does not mean the request has been approved, a quote issued, payment received or work started. It does not waive rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

ALBUKHR may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes should be reviewed, approved and communicated before being treated as operative. Commercial terms not finalized here—including payment methods, refunds, cancellation, timelines, revisions and intellectual-property arrangements—require separate approval and clear disclosure.
