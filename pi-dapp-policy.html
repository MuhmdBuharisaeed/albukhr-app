Pi dApp Launch Service Policy

This policy explains how ALBUKHR receives, reviews, quotes and coordinates requests for Pi dApp assistance. Submitting a request is not approval, a contract to start work, confirmation of payment, or a guarantee of Pi Network approval.

Request review first Quote before payment Pi payments currently disabled
Important payment notice: The service uses a quote-first process. No service fee is due merely because a request was submitted or reviewed. Payment methods, currency/asset, refunds, cancellation rules and service timelines remain subject to separate written approval and publication. Do not send Pi or bank payments based on an informal message, screenshot, receipt upload or unverified instruction.

1. Purpose and service scope

ALBUKHR may help applicants prepare, design, develop or launch a Pi Network dApp, depending on the selected service and the scope agreed in an accepted quote.

  • Pi Studio Assistance: guidance for preparing and launching through Pi Studio.
  • Developer-built dApp: a request for custom development by ALBUKHR developers or contractors.
  • App for an ALBUKHR Project: limited to eligible Internal or External projects after the server verifies the applicant’s ownership and project eligibility.

Core projects are managed by the ALBUKHR Team and are not selectable through the applicant-owned project pathway. Manager-only access is not implied unless it is explicitly enabled and verified by the service.

2. Request submission and review

Applicants must provide an accurate app/project name, a clear description of requirements, the selected service route, and the required policy consent.

  • ALBUKHR verifies the applicant’s Pi identity on the server side.
  • Project association and network scope must be validated by the service; a browser-supplied identity or project claim is not proof of ownership.
  • Administrators may request more information, decline a request, or determine that the requested scope is not feasible.
  • Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved or queued for work.

3. Quote-first workflow

The service follows this intended sequence. A step is not complete until the relevant status or decision is confirmed by ALBUKHR’s trusted service.

1 · SubmitThe applicant submits a request and accepts the applicable policy version.
2 · ReviewALBUKHR reviews feasibility, requirements and any linked project eligibility.
3 · QuoteWhere accepted for quoting, ALBUKHR issues a specific scope and price quote with its terms and validity.
4 · Applicant decisionThe applicant may accept or decline the exact quote version. Acceptance alone is not payment confirmation.

A quote should identify the agreed scope, deliverables, price and currency/asset, validity period and applicable terms. If scope or price changes, a revised quote/version must be issued; an earlier quote must not silently be altered. Work may begin only after the necessary service, payment and operational approvals are confirmed under the final published workflow.

4. Fees and payment status

There are no guaranteed or fixed prices in this policy. A fee, currency/asset, payment method, validity period and any payment conditions must be specified in an authorized quote and the final applicable terms.

  • Request submission and quote acceptance must not be represented as a completed payment.
  • Payment state is separate from request/workflow state.
  • Payment channels are disabled by default and must not be treated as available unless ALBUKHR explicitly enables and announces them after testing and approval.
  • Do not send funds to a personal wallet or bank account based only on a chat message or screenshot.

This policy does not activate Pi U2A, bank transfer, or any other payment channel.

5. Cancellation, refunds and timelines

Detailed cancellation, refund, revision limits, delivery acceptance, turnaround/service-level commitments and dispute procedures have not been finalized by this policy. They must be stated in separately approved terms or in the applicable quote before the applicant commits to them.

Applicants should not assume that a payment is refundable or non-refundable unless the final terms expressly say so. ALBUKHR must not promise a refund schedule or delivery date that has not been authorized.

6. Ownership and responsibilities

Applicants remain responsible for the accuracy and legality of the content, branding, data, permissions and third-party materials they provide. ALBUKHR does not automatically become the owner of an applicant’s dApp merely by providing assistance.

Ownership of source code, intellectual property, credentials, hosting, maintenance, handover and third-party licences must be explicitly agreed in the final written scope/terms. This policy does not itself transfer intellectual property or grant a licence not stated elsewhere.

7. Pi Network and third-party decisions

ALBUKHR can provide assistance within its agreed scope, but it cannot guarantee acceptance, listing, visibility, approval or continued availability by Pi Network or any other third party.

  • No guaranteed Pi Network Core Team approval or listing.
  • No guaranteed user adoption, revenue, investment return or Pi earnings.
  • Applicants must comply with applicable Pi Network rules, security requirements and relevant law.

8. Security, identity and records

Requests are intended to be associated with the server-verified Pi identity and the configured network. Applicants must not attempt to access another person’s requests, quotes or private service information.

ALBUKHR may keep request, quote, consent and administrative audit records for service delivery, security, dispute review and compliance. Applicants must not send passwords, private keys, Pi access tokens or other authentication secrets in request descriptions or messages.

9. Misuse and refusal

ALBUKHR may pause, decline or close a request where information is misleading, the request is unlawful or unsafe, the applicant cannot establish required authority, the scope is technically infeasible, or continued processing would conflict with ALBUKHR policy or applicable third-party rules. Where appropriate, the reason may be provided through the service workflow.

10. Policy acceptance and status

When an applicant checks the policy-consent box and submits a request, they confirm that they have read and understood this policy version. Consent records should identify the version accepted. Consent does not waive rights that cannot lawfully be waived and does not mean ALBUKHR has approved the request, issued a quote, received payment or started work.

Policy status: This page describes the current quote-first service rules and explicitly marks unresolved commercial terms as not yet finalized. Any future change to payment channels, refunds, cancellation, service timelines or ownership terms must be separately approved and communicated before it is treated as operative.
