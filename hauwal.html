About Hauwal Sumonviter

Hauwal Sumonviter is an ALBUKHR food and agro-processing initiative aimed at modernizing local maize milling into a clean, efficient, and commercially viable operation.

Funds are used for modern milling equipment, hygiene systems, packaging, storage, and distribution infrastructure.

The project creates employment, improves food quality, and strengthens community-based agricultural value chains.
