About Khairat Fertiliser

Khairat Organic Fertiliser is an ALBUKHR environmental and agricultural initiative that transforms biodegradable waste into nutrient-rich fertiliser.

Stake funds support waste collection, processing facilities, composting systems, and local farmer distribution.

The project promotes circular economy principles, reduces pollution, and increases sustainable food production.
