Introduction

ALBUKHR Investment Limited is developing a digital ecosystem intended to connect community participation, project development, transparent governance and productive real-world economic activity. The ecosystem is being built around project registration, review, visibility, investment-readiness controls and digital services, with a focus on making project information and relevant decisions easier to understand.

The company’s purpose is to support a more structured route for viable businesses and initiatives to present their plans, explain their funding needs, supply relevant evidence and progress through review. The platform is designed to distinguish a project's public information from verified evidence, administrative approval from operational activation, and project ambitions from outcomes that have actually been achieved.

ALBUKHR’s work is ongoing. Some components have application pages and governance workflows, while other services and parts of the project-market framework remain under development, testing or controlled review. This whitepaper is an updated overview of the intended mission and operating model; it does not supersede the applicable policies, project terms, service conditions or recorded decisions.

How to read this documentStatements about vision and objectives describe intended direction. Service status labels describe the evidence available at the stated review date. A planned feature must not be understood as live, available to every user or approved by Pi Network.

03

Why ALBUKHR Was Created

Many small businesses and productive initiatives need a better way to explain their purpose, identify their capital needs, present supporting evidence and build trust with potential participants. Users, in turn, need understandable information about a project’s ownership, operating stage, assumptions, risks and applicable terms before deciding whether to engage with it.

ALBUKHR is being developed to address these gaps through a governed project registry and supporting services. Its intended contribution is not to eliminate commercial risk or guarantee that every project will succeed. It is to structure information, review, disclosure and access decisions so that users can make more informed decisions.

  • Support the presentation and development of productive projects.
  • Provide a controlled framework for project applications and review.
  • Improve transparency around project status, terms and readiness.
  • Encourage responsible use of digital tools to support real economic activity.
  • Reduce confusion between project promotion, administrative approval and eligibility for financial activity.

Security, Trust & Accountability

ALBUKHR’s intended technical safeguards include shared authentication components, controlled API access, server-side authorization, project registry integrity, auditable administrative operations and separation of Mainnet and Testnet data. The implementation must continue to be reviewed as services evolve.

  • Authentication establishes an account session; it does not automatically authorize every action.
  • Privileged permissions must be enforced by trusted backend and database controls, not by a hidden button or frontend-only condition.
  • Mainnet and Testnet must remain isolated. Test records must not be used as evidence of Mainnet balances, funding or readiness.
  • Project information should distinguish evidence verified by the system from applicant declarations, assumptions and forecasts.
  • Where fund custody or liquidity verification is required, it must be supported by the applicable controls and records.
No system is risk-freeSecurity controls reduce risk but cannot remove every technical, operational, human, market or legal risk. Suspected errors and vulnerabilities should be investigated and handled through the appropriate response process.

The ALBUKHR ecosystem is organised around distinct but related layers. Public pages help users discover information and service entry points; authenticated workspaces support eligible activities; project registry records establish project identity and status; governance and review processes evaluate evidence; and backend/database controls enforce permissions and network boundaries.

Project Registry

Holds authoritative project identity and status information used by the relevant application pathways.

Project Review

Services

Administration & Audit

The interface is not the authority for financial permission or project activation. Authoritative status, permissions and financial transitions must be enforced through the corresponding trusted backend and database contracts.

Project Framework: Core, Internal and External Projects

ALBUKHR uses project-origin categories to apply the governance and review process appropriate to how a project entered the ecosystem. These categories describe project origin and governance pathways; they do not, by themselves, indicate quality, safety, investment eligibility or guaranteed funding.

Core Projects

Core Projects originate from ALBUKHR’s internally developed programme. They remain subject to the applicable ownership, funding, risk, disclosure, technical and activation requirements. A Core label alone does not authorize financial activity.

Internal / Contributor Projects

Internal Projects are created through an eligible ALBUKHR Contributor pathway or another formally authorized internal process. The current Contributor workspace describes a governed entitlement of one Internal Project for an eligible Contributor, subject to active Contributor status, profile/entitlement checks and the relevant project workflow. Registration or joining a public community alone does not grant Contributor status.

External Projects

External Projects originate with independent applicants who submit their project or business for review. The External Project framework may require identity and ownership checks, business documentation, due diligence, risk assessment, funding structure, custody controls where applicable, project terms, disclosure and technical readiness.

Approval is not activationAll project-origin categories must follow their applicable controls. A project may be visible or approved while remaining inactive and ineligible for new investment/staking activity. Project-specific policy and backend authorization determine the actual permitted pathway.

For public discovery, projects may be listed together without displaying their origin category. That presentation choice does not remove the category-specific governance requirements used behind the scenes.

Funding, Investment Readiness & Activation

ALBUKHR’s project-market design is intended to make project funding needs and the basis for any proposed financial terms more understandable. Project owners may need to explain business stage, funding purpose, capital requirements, expected use of funds, financial assumptions, operating obligations and the evidence supporting their proposal.

Funding models are not identical. A new business being built from the ground up has different evidence and execution risks from an operating business seeking expansion capital or a business needing short-cycle working capital. These differences should be reflected in project-specific review and disclosures rather than forced into one universal rate or template.

1
Project approval

The applicable administrative review has reached an approval outcome. This alone does not authorize investment.

2
Funding and economic readiness

Funding purpose, capital assumptions and relevant obligations are assessed under the applicable model.

3
Risk, liquidity and custody checks

Required controls and evidence are checked for the activity the project seeks to open.

4
Terms and disclosure

Users must be able to understand the applicable terms, assumptions, risks and limits before confirming a financial action.

5
Technical readiness and final authorization

Registry integrity, network alignment, contracts and other applicable controls are checked before an authorized activation.

The precise production behavior depends on the applicable approved contract, policy and implementation. The related Project Market framework documents are controlled design drafts at the time of this update and should be described accordingly.

Technology, Privacy & Network Isolation

ALBUKHR uses a shared application architecture in which environment selection, authentication, Supabase access, page-specific logic and API calls have distinct responsibilities. The intended design uses trusted shared components and backend authorization rather than allowing each page to independently invent its own identity, database or financial controls.

  • Mainnet and Testnet must remain isolated at the configuration, query, write and transaction layers.
  • Testnet records and test transactions must never be represented as Mainnet value or evidence of production readiness.
  • Authentication and database access should use the shared ALBUKHR cores where applicable. Sensitive authority must remain server-side.
  • Personal information and evidence documents should be collected only for a defined purpose and protected through appropriate access controls, retention rules and review.
  • Project logos and metadata are part of registry integrity and should be validated under the applicable rules.

Technical implementation changes over time. This whitepaper describes architectural principles, not a warranty that every page or integration has passed an independent security audit. Users should not provide passwords, private keys or recovery phrases to project applicants or service staff.

Risk, Liquidity & User Protection

Participation in a business or project may involve commercial, financial, operational, liquidity, technical, governance, fraud and legal/regulatory risks. An approval badge does not mean a project is risk-free or that ALBUKHR guarantees profitability, principal repayment, reward payment or exit liquidity.

The ALBUKHR design documents call for project-specific risk assessment, appropriate disclosure, separation of project operating resources from restricted user resources, and controls for treasury, liquidity and obligations where the project activity requires them. Those standards must be implemented, approved and tested before being represented as fully operational controls.

  • Project declarations, verified facts, management assumptions and forecasts should be clearly distinguished.
  • Unverified balances must not be described as verified liquidity or ALBUKHR-held funds.
  • Custody, escrow, segregation and controlled release are required where the approved project model calls for them; approval alone does not mean ALBUKHR holds project funds.
  • Terms, risk disclosures and conditions should be available before a user confirms an investment-like action.
  • Material changes may require a new review, disclosure update, restriction or suspension.
No guaranteed returnsNo service card, project registration, approval, Contributor status or token-related plan should be treated as a guarantee of return, liquidity, capital protection, funding or future market value.

ALB Points & Future ALB Token

ALBUKHR is planning a points-first engagement and rewards service. The initial concept is to award points for eligible actions within the ALBUKHR app or website, with anti-abuse controls and an auditable reward record. A Telegram entry point may be considered later, using the same authoritative reward service if approved.

Points are not a blockchain token

Points are planned as internal reward records. They are not inherently cryptocurrency, do not automatically have a monetary value, are not necessarily transferable and do not create an automatic right to Pi, cash or a future token. No points-mining system is represented here as currently active.

Possible future ALB Token

ALB Token is a future concept, not an issued asset in this draft. Any decision to convert points into a token must be defined by a separate approved policy and tokenomics design. It must address eligibility, supply and allocation, anti-fraud rules, user disclosure, technical security and the treatment of points that do not qualify.

Pi Launchpad and market price

ALBUKHR intends to investigate an official Pi Network pathway if an appropriate Mainnet route becomes available and ALBUKHR meets all applicable conditions. Pi Launchpad access, token creation and market availability must not be assumed. No token price, exchange listing, convertibility, liquidity or profit is guaranteed by ALBUKHR.

Current service statusALB Token & Points Rewards is marked “In Development” and “Not Open Yet” in the Services page. No mining, token issuance, conversion, trading or payments are enabled through that service.
