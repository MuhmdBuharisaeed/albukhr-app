01

Purpose

This Policy establishes the minimum governance, integrity, disclosure, funding, custody, technical and operational requirements for an independent project seeking participation in the ALBUKHR External Project framework.

Important: Submission does not create a right to approval, funding, listing, activation or continued participation.
02

External Project

An External Project is a project or business originating outside ALBUKHR's internally originated project programme and submitted by an independent applicant for review.

External Project status does not mean that ALBUKHR owns the project, guarantees its performance, guarantees returns or accepts the applicant's business obligations.

03

Core Principles

External Projects must operate according to:

Truthfulness and completeness
Evidence-based review
Ownership and authority verification
Investor protection and material-risk disclosure
Appropriate custody and fund segregation
Strict Mainnet/Testnet isolation
Separation of approval from activation
No guaranteed-return or guaranteed-performance representations
04

Applicant and Ownership

Applicants must use an authenticated ALBUKHR identity and have authority to submit the project. ALBUKHR may verify applicant identity, ownership authority, business registration, business identity, location, operating status, legal/regulatory dependencies and relevant ownership relationships.

05

Evidence and Documents

Depending on the project, ALBUKHR may require:

  • business registration
  • ownership documentation
  • identity documentation
  • business plan or project proposal
  • financial statements
  • operating evidence
  • licences or permits
  • commercial evidence
  • funding evidence
  • custody/account-control evidence
  • technical/project-registry evidence
Prohibited: False, fabricated, materially misleading or unauthorized documents are prohibited.
06

Due Diligence

Due diligence may examine:

  1. owner identity
  2. ownership authority
  3. business registration
  4. business identity
  5. business location
  6. operating status
  7. legal/regulatory dependencies
  8. related-party conflicts
  9. business evidence

Missing, inconsistent or unverifiable evidence may block progression.

07

Risk Assessment

Relevant risk dimensions include:

Business ModelExecutionFinancial/EconomicLiquidityOperationalOwnership/GovernanceFraud/Market IntegrityLegal/Regulatory

A formal risk band must use a documented methodology. A risk classification is not a guarantee of safety or investment performance.

08

Capital and Funding

Applicants must distinguish:

Capital Requirementoverall project funding need
Required Liquidityliquidity determined or approved by the ALBUKHR framework
Verified Liquidityliquidity actually verified through the applicable process
These are not interchangeable. Declared liquidity is not automatically verified liquidity. Existing assets are not automatically liquidity.

Funding must be reasonably itemized and may include CAPEX, OPEX, working capital, reserve, contingency and other applicable categories. ALBUKHR may revise or reject requested amounts.

09

Custody and Fund Controls

Where required by the project model, custody, escrow, segregation, controlled release, reconciliation or other fund-control mechanisms must be established and verified.

Approval does not mean ALBUKHR holds project funds. Unverified balances must not be represented as verified ALBUKHR custody or liquidity.

10

Terms and Disclosure

Approved project terms may define investment model, asset, target capital, investment limits, duration, funding window, return model, principal policy, exit, fees, distributions, default policy and conditions.

Material disclosure must distinguish verified facts, project declarations, forecasts and assumptions, and must disclose material risks, conflicts, dependencies, limitations and relevant terms.

Terms approvalDisclosure publicationActivation

These are separate controls.

11

Technical Readiness

Before activation, applicable checks may cover:

  • application/project binding
  • network alignment
  • registry integrity
  • owner binding
  • project metadata
  • project logo
  • registry uniqueness

Technical readiness does not itself authorize activation.

12

Activation

The External lifecycle is:

Application→Ownership Verification→Due Diligence→Risk Assessment→Funding Structure→Custody Controls Where Required→Terms→Disclosure→Funding Readiness→Technical Readiness→ACTIVE
Approval is not activation. External Projects cannot be made ACTIVE by a frontend user or direct status update; activation must pass the authoritative server-side gate.
13

Prohibited Conduct

Fabricated or false information
Impersonation
Falsified registration or ownership
Manipulated financial evidence
Misrepresentation of unverified funds
Concealment of material conflicts or liabilities
Unauthorized ALBUKHR endorsement/guarantee claims
Guaranteed-return claims on behalf of ALBUKHR
Bypassing review, custody, disclosure or activation controls
Unauthorized database/API manipulation
Mainnet/Testnet mixing
Malicious uploads
Unauthorized third-party documents
14

Review Outcomes

ALBUKHR may approve, request revision, reject, defer, block, require additional evidence, require mitigation, revise terms, revise disclosure, or require additional custody controls.

15

Material Changes

Material changes to ownership, business identity, investment model, funding structure, project purpose, custody, risk profile, terms or legal/regulatory position may require re-review, revised disclosure or suspension.

16

Monitoring and Records

ALBUKHR may retain application, evidence, review, risk, funding, custody, terms, disclosure, technical and activation records for governance, security, audit and ecosystem operations.

17

No Automatic Investment Relationship

Application, approval, conversion or activation does not by itself create a partnership, agency relationship, ownership transfer, guaranteed financing, guaranteed investment, guaranteed return or guaranteed liquidity.

18

Acceptance

Applicants may be required to confirm that they have read and understood this Policy, have authority to submit, will provide accurate information, understand that approval is not activation, and agree to applicable due-diligence, risk, funding, custody, disclosure, technical and monitoring requirements.

19

Related Standards

This Policy should be read together with the applicable ALBUKHR standards for Project Market Activation, Risk, Capital and Funding, Liquidity and Treasury, Disclosure, and External Project technical controls.

Important: This is an ALBUKHR ecosystem governance policy, not legal, accounting, tax, investment or regulatory advice.
